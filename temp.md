# How to tweak

Horizontal movement:
- High Accel to a Top Speed
- Fairly high Decel, and high turn speed

Air movement:
- Air brake
- Air control
- Top jump
- Stronger down gravity than up gravity?
- Variable jump (at some point)


Rule of thumb: SET acceleration from movement. ADD to velocity variable.
